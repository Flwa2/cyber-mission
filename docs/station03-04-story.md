# Station 03 to 04 narrative refinement

## Task 1.1 readability follow-up

New story-version-3 snapshots retain the narrative below but simplify the technical
copy. The version-2 email samples and technical timeline below are an editorial
reference, not a verbatim copy of every current preview. WS-31 becomes “Project
review computer” / “جهاز مراجعة المشروع”; PL-85 remains internal while
Project_Sept.zip connects the visible records. External sharing is described as
sharing outside the organization. A separate contact sheet links the work computer
to an employee; the sharing record does not name the intended answer. Ordinary
sender addresses and useful filenames remain authentic. Raw evidence IDs, account
addresses, device IDs, references, timestamps and scoring fields remain intact.

Only newly initialized narrative snapshots receive this copy. Historical snapshots
are not migrated. `validateIncidentStory.mjs` checks code-free visible evidence,
unchanged internal facts and historical preservation; `validateReadableEvidence.mjs`
renders 32 bilingual mail/log/device/document views to check localized values.

## Key clues and fair distractions

- Contacts plus the activity account/device connect a selected profile to a session.
- Matching transfer reference and filename connect preparation, download and the
  external activity. A delivery receipt or deleted sharing receipt corroborates it.
- The printed pack and badge are circumstantial. Preparing a pack, carrying a badge
  or requesting a meeting copy does not establish that someone made an external share.
- Invoice_1048.pdf is a revised invoice with unchanged amounts. A request to remove
  an old draft is plausible routine work, distinct from deleting a sharing receipt.
- Meeting-room and site-reading updates provide ordinary activity. Neither is
  presented as an alibi or proof of wrongdoing.
- Station 04 retains only actual Station 02 decisions and final selected Station 03
  evidence, including irrelevant choices. Removed/uncollected clues are not inserted.

## Compatibility and limitations

- Existing bound investigation snapshots are not rewritten, including already-read
  or completed missions. New content appears in newly initialized investigations.
  The existing saved Huda mission therefore keeps its earlier content.
- Station 02 physical ownership/print notes remain unchanged; these are background
  custody/preparation details, not a replacement for the Station 03 account activity.
- The three-person assignment remains independent of the four historical case
  templates. Narrative display accounts are bound to that already-saved answer;
  the answer itself, case IDs and all relevance/scoring rules stay unchanged.
- No claim is made about who physically used an account or whether they intended harm.
- The isolated Station 04 preview still uses its small development fixture; it is
  not a complete investigator's case. Use a fresh connected mission to review the
  full new narrative. No live mission was reset or edited for this task.
